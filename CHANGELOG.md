# What's new in Thock

## v1.1.0 — 2026-11-24

A new look, in the same family as Khalid Khalil's other apps.

- New: a pixel-art app icon and menu bar icon.
- New: a pixel launch animation. The key drops in, lights up, and "THOCK" types itself out to the sound of your pack.
- New: Settings → Appearance. Pick Pixel (Thock's own look) or Classic (standard macOS icons and font), and any accent color.
- Improved: a new Settings window with a sidebar and grouped cards, and a new About page.
- Improved: a new installer window. Drag Thock to Applications, with the first-launch steps right there.

## v1.0.2 — 2026-11-23

- Changed: new contact email, info@khalidkhalil.dev.

## v1.0.1 — 2026-11-23

- Fixed: the menu bar panel now opens at its full size. Before, it could open too short and cut off its header and the Settings / Quit row.

## v1.0 — 2026-11-23

First release.

- 16 mechanical keyboard sound packs, with stereo by key position, pitch variety and a clarity filter.
- Key strength read from the MacBook's motion sensor: soft, medium, hard or slam.
- A boom on a slammed Enter, and Shouting mode.
